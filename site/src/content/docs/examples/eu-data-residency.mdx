The rule matches operations whose data class is `personal` or `sensitive` and whose destination region is not `EU`, and denies them. If the region cannot be determined the operation is denied as well (fail closed). Region identifiers are organizational strings.

See [Policy-as-Code](/governance/policy-as-code/).

AWP provides machine-readable governance primitives that can help organizations implement and enforce their own compliance requirements. Compliance depends on the implementation, organizational controls and applicable regulatory requirements.
